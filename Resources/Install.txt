SOFAONMAC – an armchair for your Mac desktop
============================================

Just throw your desktop clutter on the sofa, like at home.
Goes well with Rugonmac: the chair then stands on the rug.
(Deutsch: siehe Anleitung.txt)

INSTALL – THE FASTEST WAY (1 minute, only once)

1. Drag Sofaonmac.app into the Applications folder.

2. Open Terminal:
   press cmd + space, type "Terminal", press Enter.

3. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal (cmd + V) and press Enter:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Sofaonmac.app

   Nothing is printed – that's how it should be. You can close Terminal.

4. Double-click Sofaonmac. Done.

WHY THIS IS NEEDED
Everything that reaches your Mac via WhatsApp, AirDrop, mail or a browser is
put into "quarantine" by macOS. For apps that aren't registered with Apple
(that costs the developer 99 € a year), macOS then refuses to open them with
"Apple could not verify ..." or "... is damaged".
The line from step 3 only removes that mark from Sofaonmac – it changes
nothing else on your Mac.

Without Terminal (takes a little longer):
Double-click Sofaonmac once and close the warning with "Done".
Then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll all the way down,
click "Open Anyway" next to Sofaonmac and confirm with your Mac password.
(If the message says "is damaged", only the Terminal way works.)

HOW TO USE IT
- An armchair stands at the bottom right of your desktop.
- Drag files, folders, pictures, links or text onto the chair:
  they land on the pile and disappear from the desktop.
  As soon as you drag something, the chair comes to the front.
- Click the chair: everything fans out.
    Click a card      = open the file
    "Put back"        = the file goes back to where it was
    Drag a card out   = drop it anywhere else
- You can move the chair itself with the mouse.
- In the menu bar there is a small chair icon:
  pick the chair and its size, set it up or put it away,
  and put everything back.
- Settings (menu > Settings …): always in front, sofa folder,
  start at login.

AFTER A RESTART
Everything on the sofa stays there, even when the Mac is off.
As soon as Sofaonmac runs again, the chair is back with everything on it.
If the app is in the Applications folder, it starts automatically at login
(can be turned off in the settings).

Nothing gets lost: everything on the sofa is in the sofa folder
(Settings > Sofa folder > In Finder), even when the app isn't running.

macOS may ask once whether Sofaonmac may access the Desktop folder -> allow.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
